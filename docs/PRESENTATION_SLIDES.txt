  • Classic benchmark for unsupervised learning


Fashion-MNIST

  • 60,000 training + 10,000 test samples
  • Fashion items (10 categories), 28×28 grayscale images
  • More complex visual patterns than MNIST


Data Preprocessing: Normalized to [0,1], flattened to 784D vectors

SLIDE 4: BASELINES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Biologically-Inspired Baselines


1. FlyHash
   • Inspired by Drosophila olfactory system
   • Random projection, no training required
   • Performance: NMI = 0.55 (MNIST)


2. Krotov-Hopfield ⭐ (Best Performance)
   • Competing hidden units with anti-Hebbian learning
   • Training: 200 epochs (~15 minutes)
   • Performance: NMI = 0.58 (MNIST)


3. SoftHebb
   • Soft winner-take-all with Hebbian plasticity
   • Training: 10 epochs (~10 minutes)
   • Performance: NMI = 0.18 (MNIST), 0.41 (Fashion-MNIST)


4. Diehl & Cook (STDP-SNN)
   • Spiking neural networks with spike-timing-dependent plasticity
   • Currently under debugging

Performance Comparison (MNIST)


Method          NMI      ARI      ACC      Training Time
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Krotov ⭐       0.5837   0.4683   0.6313   ~15 minutes
FlyHash         0.5494   0.4089   0.5748   instant
SoftHebb        0.1806   0.0878   0.2094   ~10 minutes


Key Findings:

  ✓ Learned features outperform random projection (+6.2% on NMI)
  
  ✓ Krotov-Hopfield achieves best clustering with efficient training
  
  ✓ Bio-inspired learning is both effective and practical for
    unsupervised clustering tasks
